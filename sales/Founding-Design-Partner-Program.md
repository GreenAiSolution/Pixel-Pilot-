# PHX Growth — Founding Design-Partner Program

> **The one job of this document:** turn a beautiful pre-revenue platform into a business with its **first paying proof client** — honestly, and fast.
> Direction: **DTC autonomous media buyer.** Stage: pre-proof, engine still simulation-first.
> Strategy: land **3 founding partners** on a no-risk, concierge-delivered pilot → produce **one real margin number** → use it to sell everyone else. Created 2026-07-15.

---

## 0 · The honest premise (read this first)

You have a stunning storefront and no receipts. Sophisticated buyers can smell a simulation. So we **don't** sell them a finished autonomous buyer — we invite a tiny founding cohort to **co-build** it with us, on terms where *they carry none of the risk and we carry all of it.* That's the only offer that's both truthful and irresistible at this stage.

The output we're buying with this program isn't revenue. It's **one sentence:** *"We took [Brand] from X to Y contribution margin in 60 days — here's the flight log."* That sentence is worth more than the next $50k of MRR, because it unlocks all of it.

## 1 · The offer — "Founding Flight Program"

**Limited to 3 brands. Ever. (Founding cohort — then the door closes and price goes to rack rate.)**

| Lever | Founding Flight | Why it disarms every objection |
|---|---|---|
| **Retainer** | **$0 for 60 days** (normally $2,500–$15k/mo) | Removes "what's it cost / is it worth it" entirely |
| **Performance fee** | **Only a share of *incremental* profit** above their own trailing baseline — nothing if we don't beat it | "We literally cannot cost you money" |
| **Setup** | **$1,000** (refundable if we don't hit the success bar) | Skin in the game so they show up, but risk-free |
| **Delivery** | **Concierge — Jaden + the PHX system, hands-on**, not a login | Honest: you get a media buyer, not unproven software |
| **Founding rate** | Whatever we agree becomes **their price for life** | Scarcity + loyalty; rewards the early bet |
| **Their ask in return** | A **case study, a testimonial, and one reference call** if we hit the bar | Fair trade — this is the whole point |

**Term:** 60-day flight, one clear success metric (below). At day 60 they convert to a founding-rate retainer or walk, no hard feelings.

**What makes it a no-brainer:** they risk $1,000 (refundable) to get a hands-on media buyer optimizing to *real profit* for two months, and they only ever pay more if you *make* them more. The downside is capped at basically zero; the upside is a better P&L.

## 2 · The success metric (define it, or you have no proof)

Pick **one**, agreed in writing before the flight, measured against *their own* trailing 60-day baseline on the spend we manage:

- **Primary:** Contribution margin % (revenue − COGS − ad spend − fees) on managed spend beats baseline.
- **Or:** MER / blended ROAS improvement at equal-or-higher spend.
- **Guardrail (honesty):** measured incrementally where possible, not last-click vanity — this *is* your Attribution Truth pitch, lived.

If you hit it → you have your case study. If you don't → you learned what to fix before you charged anyone real money. Both outcomes advance the business. That's why this step is unskippable.

## 5 · The 10-day sprint to first proof

1. **Days 1–2:** Finalize the offer terms + the one success metric. Set up the $1k setup invoice (Stripe) and a one-page pilot agreement (see `Legal + billing spine` — build next).
2. **Days 2–3:** Send **client-track** messages to the 5 realistic targets (email + X DM). Send **advisor-track** to Sean Frank + Nik Sharma the same day (their reply warms the client ones).
3. **Days 3–7:** Book calls. Goal: **3 founding-partner yeses.** Over-ask (message 8–10 brands beyond the 15 if needed — I can pull more).
4. **Day 7:** First partner signs → get ad-account access (the `Pilot delivery runbook` covers exactly how).
5. **Days 7–60:** Fly it concierge. Log everything. Weekly one-line update to the partner.
6. **Day ~60:** Produce the flight-log case study → that sentence → repoint all future outreach at it.

## 6 · What this unlocks (why it's THE next step)

The moment you have one real margin number, every other piece of "a complete built business" becomes buildable on solid ground:
- **Legal/billing spine** → now worth setting up, because money is real.
- **Making the core real** (real Meta/Google/TikTok APIs) → now *justified*, because a paying client is waiting on it.
- **Repeatable acquisition** → the 15-contact dossier stops asking people to trust a simulation and starts showing them a result.

You don't need to build all of it. You need to build **the proof first** — everything else has been waiting on it.

---

### Guardrails (keep it honest)
- Never claim results you don't have yet. The founding-partner framing is powerful *because* it's truthful: "co-build with me at no risk," not "buy my proven product."
- Only take a % of **incremental, real** profit — measured against their baseline. That integrity is literally the product.
- Cap the cohort at 3. You can only concierge-deliver a few, and scarcity is real here, not a tactic.
